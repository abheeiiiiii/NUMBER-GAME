How to Play

Clear the board by finding and joining pairs of numbers.

Valid Pairs

  • Numbers are the same (e.g., 7 and 7).
  • Numbers add up to 10 (e.g., 2 and 8).

Joining Rules

You can join a pair if the path between them is clear of other numbers in any of these directions:

  • Horizontally (side-by-side)
  • Vertically (above or below)
  • Diagonally
  • Line-by-Line: The end of one row can connect to the start of the next.
